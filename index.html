Shortcut Factory · Unofficial community

🏭 welcome to shortcut factory! ⚙️

shortcut factory is a community centered around apple shortcuts, where creators can build, share, test, discuss, and showcase their creations.

🔧 build

share shortcut projects, get help with difficult ideas, and work on new creations.

📤 share

post your shortcuts in dedicated channels for games, utilities, and other projects.

💡 ideas

got an idea for a shortcut? drop it in and let the community help develop it.

🧪 testing

test unfinished shortcuts, find bugs, and improve projects before releasing them.

🏆 showcase

show off your best creations and see what other members have built.

💬 community

introduce yourself, chat on the factory floor, welcome new members, or just hang out.

⭐ feedback

use the review and submission areas to give feedback and help improve the factory.

🎤 events

join scheduled stage events, discussions, showcases, and other live activities.

whether you’re making a tiny shortcut or a completely ridiculous 1,000+ action monster, there’s room on the factory floor. 💀

build it. test it. ship it. 🏭⚙️
